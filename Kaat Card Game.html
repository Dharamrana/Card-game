Kaat

Four-player card table

Quick rules
Each player gets 13 cards. Every hand is redealt until all four players hold at least one card of every suit. The caller chooses a kaat suit, starts the first trick, and bids at least 6 ghar. Other players bid at least 2. Follow the lead suit if you can. If you cannot, play kaat or any card. Kaat beats non-kaat. Without kaat, the highest card in the lead suit wins. Rank order is A, K, Q, J, 10 down to 2. The trick winner takes one ghar and leads next. Make your bid or more for plus bid points; miss it for minus bid points. When total bids are below 14, the table raises bids by one in seat order until total bids reach at least 14. First to +21 wins.
A local game for one player and three computer opponents. No account or network needed.
